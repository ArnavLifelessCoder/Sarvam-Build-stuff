How it works

Three steps, and you're off the phone.

The calls nobody wants to make — chasing the tailor, booking a cylinder, checking a clinic slot — usually take ten minutes and a language you don't quite speak. This takes the call for you.

01

Say what you need

Type it, or just say it in any Indian language. Add anything it's allowed to share — your budget, a visit time, a consumer number.

02

Check the plan

It writes the questions it needs answered and its opening line. Edit, remove or add anything before it dials.

03

It calls, you read

It holds the conversation in their language, ticks off each answer, and gives you a summary you can forward on WhatsApp.

Two ways to call

Use your phone, or let Indus dial.

Speakerphone mode works the moment you add a Sarvam API key. Real calls run on Indus Voice Agents, Sarvam's own telephony stack, so nothing touches your phone at all.

Speakerphone

Dial on your phone, put it on speaker next to your laptop. The AI listens, thinks and talks through the laptop — and you can step in any time.

Guardrails

Helpful, not overeager.

It's calling on your behalf, so it behaves like a careful assistant would — polite, honest about who it is, and unwilling to commit you to anything.

Never pays or shares OTPsAsked for an advance? It says it'll confirm with you first.
Only shares what you allowYour address, numbers and plans stay out unless you wrote them in.
Honest about who it isIt introduces itself as your assistant and says it's an AI if asked.
You stay in chargeTake over mid-call, or whisper an instruction it acts on without them hearing.
